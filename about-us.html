Our beginning

Built to remove the middleman.

Carchope was founded in 2017 to provide consignment services. The business connects car buyers directly with sellers, reducing middleman involvement, hassle and unnecessary cost.

Today, Carchope continues to provide consignment services at zero cost while also maintaining a fleet of cars and focusing on competitive pricing and service throughout the ownership journey.
